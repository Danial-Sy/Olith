# Connection logos

These are the provider logos used in Connections. They are bundled with the app and
belong to their respective owners.

- `github.svg`: white Invertocat from [GitHub's logo assets](https://brand.github.com/foundations/logo), `GitHub_Logos.zip`.
- `vscode.svg`: stable icon from [Visual Studio Code's icon assets](https://code.visualstudio.com/brand), `visual-studio-code-icons.zip`.
- `google.png`: G logo from [Google Identity's branding assets](https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines), `static/identity/images/g-logo.png`.
- `microsoft.svg`: symbol from [Microsoft's sign-in branding assets](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/identity-platform/howto-add-branding-in-apps), `ms-symbollockup_mssymbol_19.svg`.

These four original assets are unmodified. Preserve their proportions and colors.

The other SVG brand marks (except Canva) come from [Simple Icons
16.36.0](https://www.npmjs.com/package/simple-icons/v/16.36.0), under CC0-1.0. The full
license is in `LICENSE-simple-icons.md` and each mark's upstream source and colors are
recorded in `simple-icons-sources.json`. Their paths and proportions are unchanged.
Logos that are hard to see on a dark background use a light single-color fill.

Additional original assets, used without modification:

- `canva.svg`: [Canva's below-50px icon](https://www.canva.dev/_next/static/media/below-50px.75fd91c0.svg), from its [developer brand guidelines](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/rest-apis/brand-guidelines/).
- `slack.png`: [Slack's site icon](https://a.slack-edge.com/9cc0056/img/icons/favicon-32.png), linked by its [media kit](https://slack.com/media-kit).
- `monday.png`: [Monday's touch icon](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/656da6fea306219773d04208/65af6bd6e742d497b5f23f69_645898132bbaac20f1963919_256x256.png), linked by [monday.com](https://monday.com/).

`custom.svg` is Olith's generic connection symbol for custom websites and servers, under
the repository's AGPL-3.0-or-later license. The app does not fetch connection logos from
outside servers.
